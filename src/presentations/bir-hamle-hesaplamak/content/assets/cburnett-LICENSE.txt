The king and queen drawings in ../components/graphics/chess-board/piece.tsx are adapted from
Colin M. L. Burnett's (Cburnett) chess artwork, as used by Lichess's cburnett set.
The adaptation changes proportions, coordinates, colors and stroke treatment.

Source:
https://github.com/lichess-org/lila/blob/master/public/piece/cburnett/wK.svg
https://github.com/lichess-org/lila/blob/master/public/piece/cburnett/wQ.svg
https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Chess_klt45.svg
https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Chess_qlt45.svg
https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/User:Cburnett/GFDL_images/Chess
